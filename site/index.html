US & Europe

Duties and compliance for the US and Europe, handled before your goods ship

The US and Europe are our main markets. Import rules in both changed in 2025–2026. Here is what matters for pearl jewelry, and what we do about it.

United States

US · CBP
  • No more de minimisUSD 800

    Since 29 August 2025, low-value commercial parcels from every country lose duty-free entry. Each shipment is declared and dutied.

    We quote DDP: duties and clearance are in the price, so your customer pays nothing on delivery.

  • Pearl disclosureFTC Jewelry Guides

    The FTC Jewelry Guides require cultured pearls to be described as cultured and treatments such as bleaching or dyeing to be disclosed.

    Our invoices and listing data state pearl type and treatment, ready to use in your store.

  • California Prop 65Pb · Cd

    Jewelry sold into California must meet limits for lead and cadmium in metal parts, or carry a warning.

    For orders that need a report, we send metal findings to a third-party lab such as SGS or Intertek for lead and cadmium testing, quoted separately.

European Union / UK

EU · UK
  • €150 duty relief ends2026-07-01

    From 1 July 2026, parcels to EU consumers pay a flat €3 duty per product category, an interim rule planned to run until 2028. VAT is handled through IOSS.

    We quote landed cost, and dropship orders can be declared under your IOSS number.

  • Nickel, lead, cadmiumREACH · EN 1811

    REACH Annex XVII limits nickel release from parts in prolonged skin contact to 0.5 µg/cm²/week, and restricts lead and cadmium in jewelry. The UK applies the same limits under UK REACH.

    On request, we send ear wires, clasps and other metal parts to a third-party lab for EN 1811 nickel release testing, quoted separately.

  • Product safetyGPSR

    Products sold to EU consumers need an EU-based responsible person and must carry manufacturer and safety information.

    We supply manufacturer details, labels and test documents for you or your EU responsible person to keep on file.

General information only, not legal advice. Duty rates change with policy; your quote at the time of order is what applies.

FAQ

Common questions

Are your pearls natural or cultured?

All our pearls are cultured, freshwater and saltwater (Akoya, South Sea, Tahitian). We do not sell imitation or shell pearls.

Where do you ship from, and how long does it take?

We ship worldwide from Zhuji, Zhejiang. In-stock items are processed in 1–3 business days. One logistics partner handles export clearance, international freight, import customs and final delivery, so you get a single tracking number. US orders can go DDP so the buyer pays no duty on arrival. Delivery to major US and European destinations usually takes 9–15 days; we quote exact transit times per destination and service.

Can I order samples first?

Yes, and we recommend it for wholesale and custom orders. Samples and their shipping are paid separately.

Why do I pay a Hong Kong company?

OCEAN BULE (HK) LIMITED is Tianqi Jewelry's Hong Kong affiliate. It handles contracts and foreign-currency payments for international orders, which keeps wire transfers and PayPal simple. If your purchasing process requires a mainland China supplier, Shenzhen Tianqi Jewelry Co., Ltd. can invoice you directly. Just tell us before you order.

What if there is a problem with my order?

Send us photos or a video after delivery. High-value items are returned and handled; for low-value items we usually reship or refund without asking you to send them back.
